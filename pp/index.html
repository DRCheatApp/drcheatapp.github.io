Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 11, 2026

DRCheat ("DRCheat", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect through the DRCheat website, how we use it, and the choices available to you.

This Privacy Policy applies to the DRCheat website and its associated website services. It does not currently cover information that may be collected through the DRCheat application or other services that may be introduced in the future.


Information we collect

We currently collect limited information through the DRCheat website.

Newsletter information

If you subscribe to our newsletter, we collect your email address and name. This information is provided voluntarily when you submit the newsletter subscription form.

We use this information to send newsletters, announcements, software updates, and other communications related to DRCheat.

You may unsubscribe from the newsletter at any time using the unsubscribe mechanism provided in our emails or through the unsubscribe page provided on the Website.


Website analytics

We use Umami Analytics to understand how visitors use the DRCheat website and to improve the Website.

Analytics may include information such as pages viewed, referring websites, browser type, operating system, device type, approximate geographic location, and general website usage and interaction information.

We use analytics information to understand website traffic, identify popular content, improve the Website, and determine which features and information are useful to visitors.

We do not use analytics to intentionally identify individual visitors.


How we use information

Information collected through the Website is used only for purposes reasonably related to operating and improving DRCheat.

This may include:

  • Sending requested newsletter communications
  • Managing newsletter subscriptions
  • Understanding website usage
  • Improving website content, functionality, and performance
  • Maintaining and securing the Website and its services

We do not sell your personal information.

We do not use newsletter information for unrelated purposes without obtaining appropriate consent or otherwise being permitted to do so by law.


Third-party services

We use third-party services to operate portions of the Website and its services.

Newsletter information may be processed through our newsletter management and email delivery infrastructure.

Website analytics are provided through Umami Analytics.

Third-party services may process information on our behalf where necessary to provide these services. We take reasonable steps to ensure that personal information handled by service providers is appropriately protected.


Data retention

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes for which it was collected, including providing requested services, maintaining records, resolving disputes, and meeting legal or security requirements.

Newsletter information may be retained while you remain subscribed or as otherwise necessary for legitimate administrative or legal purposes.

You may request that your personal information be removed where applicable.


Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational measures to protect information from unauthorized access, use, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

However, no method of transmission or storage can be guaranteed to be completely secure. You should therefore avoid submitting sensitive or confidential information through forms on this Website unless specifically requested.


Your privacy rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to personal information we hold about you, including the ability to:

  • Ask what personal information we hold about you
  • Request access to your personal information
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of information where applicable
  • Withdraw consent for certain uses of your information
  • Ask questions or raise concerns about our privacy practices

To make a privacy-related request, please contact us using the contact information provided on the DRCheat Website.

We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.


Newsletter unsubscribing

You can unsubscribe from DRCheat newsletter communications at any time.

Unsubscribing will stop future newsletter communications, although we may retain limited information necessary to record your unsubscribe request and prevent further unwanted communications.


Children's privacy

The DRCheat Website is not intended to collect unnecessary personal information from children.

We do not knowingly request unnecessary personal information from children.


Changes to this privacy policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes to the Website, services, technology, or applicable requirements.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this page will be updated.

We encourage visitors to review this page periodically.


Contact

If you have questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or the handling of your personal information, please contact DRCheat using the contact information provided on the Website.
