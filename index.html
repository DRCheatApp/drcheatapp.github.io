FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about DRCheat.

What is DRCheat? +

DRCheat is an all-in-one toolkit built for NDT technicians and students. It combines reference tables, calculators, cheat sheets, image tools, productivity features, and more into one lightweight Windows application.

Who is DRCheat for? +

DRCheat is designed for NDT technicians, trainees, students, and anyone working in the field who needs quick access to important information and tools.

Which NDT methods does DRCheat support? +

DRCheat is designed to support multiple NDT methods, including methods such as Digital Radiographic Testing (DRT), Real Time Radiography (RTR), Ultrasonic Testing (UT), and other common inspection workflows.

Does DRCheat work offline? +

Yes. Most features work completely offline. Internet access is only required for features such as Riddy AI, licensing, updates, and online communication through the Connect system.

What operating systems are supported? +

DRCheat is currently available exclusively for Windows devices.

How much storage space does DRCheat require? +

DRCheat is designed to stay lightweight and requires less than 1GB of storage space for installation.

Is DRCheat subscription-based? +

Yes. DRCheat uses a subscription model per device, giving users access to updates, improvements, and ongoing feature development.

Do you offer company licenses? +

Yes. Company and team licensing options are available for organizations that need multiple devices or users.

How do I install DRCheat? +

Installation is simple. Download the installer, follow the setup steps, and DRCheat will be ready to use.

How do I receive updates? +

DRCheat includes a built-in updater that makes it easy to install new versions and receive the latest improvements.

Does DRCheat include AI features? +

Yes. DRCheat includes Riddy AI, an assistant designed to help with questions, decisions, and workflow support. Internet access is required for AI features.

Can I store my own notes and files in DRCheat? +

Yes. DRCheat includes productivity tools that allow you to manage your own notes, files, references, and other useful information.

What is the Connect System? +

The Connect System allows technicians to communicate and share information through DRCheat using local connections or the internet when available.

Can I suggest new features? +

Absolutely. Feedback and feature suggestions help shape future versions of DRCheat.

Can DRCheat replace all of my current tools? +

DRCheat is designed to reduce the number of separate tools needed during your workflow by combining commonly used references, calculations, and productivity features into one application.
